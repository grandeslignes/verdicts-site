# Privacy Policy

*Effective 11 October 2026*

> ## In short
>
> - Verdicts collects no data. No accounts, servers, ads or tracking.
> - Your game goes straight between the phones in the room and nowhere else.
> - Your name, emoji and photo stay on your phone.
> - Questions: [hello@getverdicts.app](mailto:hello@getverdicts.app)

## Full policy

### 1. Controller

The Verdicts app ("the App") is provided by Henri Tunkkari ("the Developer").

### 2. Data collection

The Developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data from use of
the App. The App contains no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and
no personal data is sold or shared.

### 3. Data on the device

The App stores the player's name, emoji, optional profile photo and a random
identifier on the device only. Deleting the App removes this data.

### 4. Data shared with other players

Games run directly between the participating devices over the local network or
Bluetooth. Other participants in a game receive the player's name, emoji,
profile photo, answers and votes, and in photo games, only the photos the
player chooses to share. Other players' profile photos are cached on the device
for up to one day. Hosted games are visible to devices on the same local
network.

### 5. Permissions

The App uses Local Network and Bluetooth to connect players, the Camera to take
a profile photo, and Photos to let players choose photos in photo games. Each
permission can be revoked in the device settings.

### 6. Crash reports

If the user has enabled sharing with app developers in the device settings,
Apple may provide the Developer with anonymous crash reports.

### 7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16.

### 8. Contact and rights

Questions and requests: [hello@getverdicts.app](mailto:hello@getverdicts.app).
Correspondence is used only to respond. Users in the EU/EEA may lodge a
complaint with a supervisory authority.

### 9. Changes

Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
