# Support

Need help or found a bug? Email
[hello@getverdicts.app](mailto:hello@getverdicts.app).

## FAQ

### How do I start a game?

One person taps **Start a game**. Everyone else opens Verdicts nearby and joins
the game that pops up.

### How many players?

3 to 24. Couples is for exactly 2.

### My friends can't see my game

- Stay close to each other with Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth turned on.
- Check that Verdicts is allowed to use the local network: **Settings ›
  Verdicts › Local Network**.

### Someone dropped out

Just open the app again. You get your seat back automatically.

### Can I try it alone?

Yes. Tap **Start a game**, then the gear, then **Try out with bots**.

### Does it cost anything?

No. Every game and every question is free.

### What about my data?

Verdicts collects no data. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

### Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. An Android version is planned.
